01. About

Fintech engineering, end to end.

Hello! I'm Patrick Mutani — a full-stack developer who lives at the intersection of fintech and engineering craft. I build secure backend systems, cross-platform mobile apps, and web solutions that handle real money for real people, every single day. It all started in 2019 when I walked into campus and decided to build my first Android app and a website from scratch. That rabbit hole of Java, XML, and HTML & CSS never let me go — and I've been building ever since.

Fast-forward to today, and I'm deep in the fintech trenches at Tangazoletu, architecting and scaling platforms that serve 70+ financial institutions. My days revolve around writing resilient microservices, shipping mobile banking features, and making sure the systems I touch are secure, performant, and built to last.

Secure backend systems

Resilient Spring Boot microservices and RESTful APIs that are secure, performant, and built to last.

Cross-platform mobile apps

Flutter and Ionic mobile banking apps for Android and iOS, shipped through Google Play Console and the Apple App Store.

Web solutions
